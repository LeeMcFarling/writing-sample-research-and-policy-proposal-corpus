# Writing Sample & Research Corpus

This is a collection of research and policy writing samples demonstrating research design, literature review, critical analysis, and applied causal-policy work. Each document is labeled by what it actually demonstrates — original research, literature review, critical analysis, or independent policy design — since these are meaningfully different kinds of evidence, and collapsing them into one undifferentiated "writing samples" folder would understate what each one is. None of the undergraduate documents are sole-authored research in the professional sense; two were produced as part of a lab group, and are marked as such in the table below.
